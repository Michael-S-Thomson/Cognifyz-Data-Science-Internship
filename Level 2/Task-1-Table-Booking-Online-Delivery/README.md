# Level 2 - Task 1: Table Booking and Online Delivery

## Objective

Analyze the relationship between table booking and online delivery services and restaurant ratings.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Table Booking Analysis

The availability of table booking was analyzed by calculating:

- Number of restaurants offering table booking
- Number of restaurants without table booking
- Percentage distribution
- Average aggregate rating based on table booking availability

### 2. Online Delivery Analysis

The availability of online delivery was analyzed by calculating:

- Number of restaurants offering online delivery
- Number of restaurants without online delivery
- Percentage distribution
- Average aggregate rating based on online delivery availability

### 3. Rating Comparison

Average Aggregate Rating was compared between restaurants:

- With and without table booking
- With and without online delivery

Bar charts were used to visualize these comparisons.

## Key Result

The dataset contains:

- **7,100 restaurants without online delivery**
- **2,451 restaurants with online delivery**

The analysis also compares average restaurant ratings based on the availability of these services.

## Conclusion

The analysis provides a descriptive comparison between service availability and restaurant ratings.

Differences in average ratings were observed between restaurants with and without table booking and between restaurants with and without online delivery.

These results indicate an association within the dataset, but they do not establish that the availability of these services causes higher or lower restaurant ratings.
