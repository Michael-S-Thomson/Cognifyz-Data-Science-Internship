# Level 2 - Task 2: Price Range Analysis

## Objective

Analyze the distribution of price ranges among restaurants and examine the relationship between price range and aggregate rating.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Price Range Distribution

The number and percentage of restaurants in each price range were calculated.

The distribution was:

* Price Range 1: 4,444 restaurants
* Price Range 2: 3,113 restaurants
* Price Range 3: 1,408 restaurants
* Price Range 4: 586 restaurants

Price Range 1 was the most common category.

### 2. Average Rating by Price Range

The average Aggregate Rating was calculated for each price range.

Price Range 4 had the highest average rating, at approximately 3.82.

### 3. Relationship Between Price Range and Rating

The correlation between Price Range and Aggregate Rating was calculated.

The correlation value was **0.438**, indicating a positive association between price range and aggregate rating in this dataset.

### 4. Visualizations

The analysis includes:

* Price range distribution bar chart
* Average rating by price range bar chart
* Aggregate rating distribution box plot

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that restaurants are distributed unevenly across the four price ranges, with Price Range 1 containing the largest number of restaurants.

Price Range 4 has the highest average Aggregate Rating at approximately 3.82.

The correlation between Price Range and Aggregate Rating is 0.438, indicating a positive association between the two variables in this dataset.

This correlation represents an association and does not establish that price range directly causes differences in restaurant ratings. Other restaurant characteristics may also influence ratings.
