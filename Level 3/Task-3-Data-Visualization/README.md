# Level 3 - Task 3: Data Visualization

## Objective

Create meaningful visualizations to identify patterns and trends in the restaurant dataset.

## Visualizations Created

### 1. Restaurant Rating Distribution
A histogram and bar chart were created to understand the distribution of restaurant ratings.

### 2. Price Range Distribution
A bar chart was used to show the number of restaurants across the four price ranges.

### 3. Customer Votes Distribution
A histogram was created to visualize the distribution of customer votes.

### 4. Top 10 Cuisines
The top 10 cuisines were identified based on the number of restaurants offering each cuisine.

### 5. Top 10 Cities
The cities with the highest number of restaurant entries were visualized.

### 6. Table Booking and Online Delivery
Separate bar charts were created to compare the availability of table booking and online delivery services.

### 7. Average Rating by Price Range
Average restaurant ratings were calculated and visualized for each price range.

## Key Findings

- Restaurant ratings have a varied distribution across the dataset.
- Price Range 1 contains the largest number of restaurants.
- North Indian is the most frequently represented cuisine.
- New Delhi has the largest number of restaurant entries.
- Online delivery and table booking are available for different proportions of restaurants.
- Price Range 4 has the highest average restaurant rating, approximately 3.82.
